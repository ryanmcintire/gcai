## EXHIBIT A — SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

**1. Uptime Commitment.** Awesomenauts will use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available with a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least **99.9%** during each calendar month (the "**Uptime Commitment**"), excluding Scheduled Maintenance and events of Force Majeure.

**2. Definitions.**
- "**Monthly Uptime Percentage**" = ((Total Minutes in Month − Unavailable Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month) × 100.
- "**Unavailable**" means the production Services are not responding to valid HTTPS requests, as measured by Awesomenauts' monitoring system, for more than five (5) consecutive minutes.
- "**Scheduled Maintenance**" means maintenance announced at least forty-eight (48) hours in advance, performed during the standard maintenance window (Saturdays, 02:00–06:00 UTC), not to exceed eight (8) hours per calendar month.

**3. Service Credits.**

| Monthly Uptime Percentage | Service Credit (% of Monthly Fees) |
|---|---|
| Less than 99.9% but ≥ 99.0% | 10% |
| Less than 99.0% but ≥ 95.0% | 25% |
| Less than 95.0% | 50% |

**4. Credit Request.** Customer must request Service Credits in writing within thirty (30) days after the end of the affected calendar month. Service Credits are applied against future invoices and are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for any failure of the Uptime Commitment, except that Customer may terminate the affected Order Form for cause if Awesomenauts fails to meet the Uptime Commitment in any three (3) months during any rolling twelve (12) month period.

**5. Support.** Awesomenauts will provide email support at support@awesomenauts.com during business hours (Monday–Friday, 6:00 AM – 6:00 PM Pacific Time, excluding U.S. federal holidays) with the following target response times:

| Severity | Description | Target Initial Response |
|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | Production Services unavailable | 1 business hour |
| Sev 2 | Major feature impaired; no workaround | 4 business hours |
| Sev 3 | Minor issue or workaround available | 1 business day |
| Sev 4 | General question or feature request | 3 business days |
